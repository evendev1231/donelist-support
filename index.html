Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I get started with Donelist?

Simply open the app and start logging anything you've done! There's no task too small. "Got out of bed," "Drank water," and "Took a deep breath" are all perfect ways to start building your momentum.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes. We take your privacy seriously. You can review our complete privacy practices by reading our Privacy Policy.

The app isn't working as expected. What should I do?

Please contact us at donelist.services@outlook.com and describe the issue in as much detail as possible (e.g., what you were doing, what you expected to happen, and what actually happened). This will help us resolve it quickly.

Can I suggest a new feature?

Absolutely! We love hearing from our users. Send us your ideas at our support email, and we'll take them into consideration for future updates.
