Frequently Asked Questions

📱 How do I sync my tasks across devices?

Answer: Tasks automatically sync across all your devices when you're signed in to iCloud. Make sure:

  • You're signed in to the same iCloud account on all devices
  • iCloud is enabled in Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud
  • You have an active internet connection

Sync happens automatically in the background - no action needed!

☁️ Why aren't my tasks syncing?

Answer: If your tasks aren't syncing, try these steps:

  1. Check that you're signed in to iCloud on all devices
  2. Verify you have an internet connection
  3. Go to Settings → iCloud Sync to check the sync status
  4. Try force-closing and reopening the app
  5. If issues persist, sign out and back in to iCloud

🔄 How do repeating tasks work?

Answer: Repeating tasks automatically regenerate based on their schedule:

  • Daily tasks: Reset every day at midnight
  • Weekly tasks: Reset on the specified day of the week
  • Monthly tasks: Reset on the specified day of the month

When you complete a repeating task, it will automatically appear again according to its schedule.

📊 How are statistics calculated?

Answer: Statistics are calculated based on:

  • Completion Rate: Completed tasks ÷ Total tasks
  • Streaks: Consecutive days with at least one completed task
  • Calendar View: Shows daily completion rates throughout the year

All calculations happen locally on your device.

📤 How do I export my data?

Answer: To export your tasks:

  1. Go to Settings → Export Data
  2. Tap the "Export Data" button
  3. Choose where to save the CSV file
  4. Your tasks will be exported in a spreadsheet-compatible format

🗑️ How do I delete my data?

Answer: To delete all your data:

  • Local data: Delete the app from your device
  • iCloud data: After deleting the app, go to Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Donelist → Delete Data

🔒 Is my data private?

Answer: Yes! Your data is completely private:

  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • iCloud sync uses your personal iCloud account
  • We do not have access to your tasks or data
  • No analytics or tracking

See our Privacy Policy for full details.
